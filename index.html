About Meso Households

The One-Stop Household Solution in Nairobi

Meso Households is your trusted household shop located in the heart of Kamukunji, Nairobi. We specialise in everything your kitchen and home needs — flasks, plates, blenders, thermos, bottles, electric kettles, cooking pots, cutlery and much more.

Whether you're setting up a new home, restocking a hotel, or just replacing that worn-out kettle, we've got you covered with durable brands, fair prices and service that treats you like family.
